Lesson 13 · Week 3Forms 2: ticks, choices and messages

LessonPracticeHomework

Write your code in the boxes. It saves by itself. When you finish, press Copy my homework and send it to your teacher on Telegram. Finished homework wins 10 diamonds for the Game Zone. Finish your homework on the same device you started on.

When I'm stuck
  1. Read my code slowly, line by line.
  2. Press Check my code and read what it says.
  3. Check that every tag that opens also closes.
  4. Look it up in My tags.
  5. Compare with the example in the lesson.
  6. Try Hint 1, then Hint 2.
  7. Still stuck? Ask your teacher.

Task 1

A quiz form: "What is 2 + 2?" with 3 radio answers (3, 4, 5) and a Check button.

Task 2

"My holiday": a drop-down of 4 countries, checkboxes for what to pack (3 things), and a textarea "Why?".

Task 3 · Your idea

Your idea! Build any page you like. Use at least 3 things from today, for example <input type="checkbox">, <input type="radio" name="size">, <select><option>Cat</option></select>, <option selected>. No idea? Try: a superhero I made up, my football team, space and planets.
