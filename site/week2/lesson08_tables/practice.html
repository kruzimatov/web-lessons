When I'm stuck
  1. Read my code slowly, line by line.
  2. Press Check my answer and look at the goals without a tick.
  3. Press Check my code and read what it says.
  4. Check that every tag that opens also closes.
  5. Look it up in My tags.
  6. Compare with the example in the lesson.
  7. Try Hint 1, then Hint 2.
  8. Still stuck? Ask your teacher.

Task 1 · Easy

A table with 2 rows: your name and age, and a friend's name and age. Use border="1".

  • A table with border="1"
  • 2 rows
  • A name and an age in each row
Hint 1

<table border="1">, then 2 <tr>.

Hint 2

Each row has 2 <td>: name and age.

Task 2 · Medium

"My pets" table with title cells Animal, Name, Age and 3 rows.

  • Title cells: Animal, Name, Age
  • 3 rows of pets
  • border="1"
Hint 1

Title row uses <th>.

Hint 2

Animal, Name, Age: 3 cells in every row.

Task 3 · Find the bug

There are 3 mistakes. Fix them.

  • Name and Goals are title cells: th
  • The first row closes: </tr>
  • 3 is in a td, not a tr
Hint 1

Does every row close? Are cells <td>? Are titles <th>?

Hint 2

Add </tr> after Goals. <tr>3</tr> should be <td>3</td>. Name and Goals are <th>.

Task 4 · Medium

Times table: title cells "x", 1, 2, 3, then rows for 2 and 3 (2 × 1 = 2 ...).

  • Title cells: x, 1, 2, 3
  • Row of 2: 2, 4, 6
  • Row of 3: 3, 6, 9
Hint 1

First row: x, 1, 2, 3 as <th>.

Hint 2

Row 2: 2, 4, 6. Row 3: 3, 6, 9.

Task 5 · Mini project

Your week timetable: Day, Morning, Evening for Monday to Friday, plus Saturday with one "Fun day!" cell using colspan="2".

  • Title cells: Day, Morning, Evening
  • Monday to Saturday
  • 6 rows of days
  • "Fun day!" with colspan="2"
Hint 1

Copy the Monday row 4 times and change the words.

Hint 2

Saturday: <td colspan="2">Fun day!</td>.
