Lesson 12 · Week 2Forms 1: boxes to type in

LessonPracticeHomework

Write your code in the boxes. It saves by itself. When you finish, press Copy my homework and send it to your teacher on Telegram. Press Check my answer on every task to win stars and diamonds. Pass all 3 for 10 bonus diamonds. Log in, and your homework saves on every device.

When I'm stuck
  1. Read my code slowly, line by line.
  2. Press Check my answer and look at the goals without a tick.
  3. Press Check my code and read what it says.
  4. Check that every tag that opens also closes.
  5. Look it up in My tags.
  6. Compare with the example in the lesson.
  7. Try Hint 1, then Hint 2.
  8. Still stuck? Ask your teacher.

Task 1

A form "Order a pizza": your name, a number box "How many?" (1 to 5) and an "Order" button.

  • A name box
  • "How many?" from 1 to 5
  • A label for each box
  • A Order button

Task 2

A form "My birthday party": name, email and the party date, all required.

  • Name, email and date boxes
  • All 3 are required
  • A label for each box

Task 3 · Your idea

Your idea! Build any page you like. Use at least 3 things from today, for example <form> ... </form>, <label for="name">Name</label>, <input type="text" id="name">, type="email" / "password" / "number" / "date". No idea? Try: my dream house, my favorite cartoon, a day at the zoo.

  • Use something new from today: button, form, input, label
  • Use 3 different tags you know
  • Write your own words
