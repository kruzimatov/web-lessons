Part 1: YouTube videos

An <iframe> is a window inside your page that shows another website. YouTube gives you one for every video.

PartMeaning
srcThe video address. It has /embed/ in it
width, heightThe size of the window
titleSays what is inside, for people who cannot see
allowfullscreenLets you watch it on the full screen
  1. On YouTube, open a video and click Share
  2. Click Embed
  3. Click Copy and paste the code into your page

iframe bu sahifa ichidagi oyna: unda boshqa sayt ko'rinadi.

Make the video window bigger: width="560" and height="315". Press play!

Part 3: Video and sound files

For a video FILE (not YouTube) use <video>. For sound use <audio>. Add controls to get the play button.

TagPlaysNeed
<video>A video file (.mp4)src, controls
<audio>A sound file (.mp3)src, controls

Press play on both. Then delete controls from the audio. What happens?

What gives you the play button?

Watch out!

  • Copying the normal YouTube link. The iframe needs the /embed/ link from Share, Embed
  • Forgetting controls: the video is there but you cannot press play
  • Forgetting </iframe>, </video> or </audio>. These tags DO need closing
  • No internet: videos and maps need the internet to load
