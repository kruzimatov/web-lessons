Part 1: Websites and tags

Which websites do you know? YouTube, Google, Roblox, Wikipedia... Every one of them is made with HTML.

A website is a file. The browser (Chrome) reads the file and shows it on the screen.

HTML is made of tags. A tag tells the browser what something is: a title, some text, a picture.

A tag is like a box: open it, put something inside, close it.

Tag bu quti: ochamiz, ichiga yozamiz, yopamiz.

PartCodeMeaning
Opening tag<h1>Open the box
ContentHello!What is inside
Closing tag</h1>Close the box. It has a /

Change Hello! to your name. Watch the right side change.

Part 2: Headings h1 to h6

Headings are titles. There are 6 of them. h1 is the biggest, h6 is the smallest.

HeadingUse it forExample
h1The page title. Only ONE on a pageMy Website
h2Big parts of the pageMy Family, My Games
h3Small parts inside a big partMy Cat, My Dog
h4 to h6Very small titles. Not used muchNotes

h1 bu kitobning nomi, h2 bu boblar, h3 bu bob ichidagi kichik qismlar.

Change the words. Then try to make an h7. What happens?

Which heading is the biggest?

Part 3: Paragraphs

Headings are for titles. For normal text we use <p>, a paragraph. Every <p> starts on a new line.

Write about your pet, your toy or your best friend: one h1 and two p.

Which tag is for normal text?

Part 4: The page skeleton and comments

A real page needs a skeleton. It is always the same. Every HTML page in the world starts like this.

PartWhat it does
<!DOCTYPE html>First line. Says: this is an HTML page
<html>The big box around everything
<head>Hidden info about the page
<title>The name on the browser tab. Goes inside head
<body>Everything you SEE: headings, text, pictures

head bu odamning boshi: ichida fikr bor, lekin ko'rinmaydi. body bu tana: hammasi ko'rinadi.

Change the h1 and the p. The title shows on the browser tab, so you cannot see it here.

A comment is a note for people. The browser does not show it. It starts with <!-- and ends with -->.

Write your own comment. Does it show on the right side?

Where do headings and text go?

Watch out!

  • Forgot the closing tag: <h1>Hello must be <h1>Hello</h1>
  • Wrong slash: <\h1> is wrong, </h1> is right
  • Different tags: <h1>Hello</h2> is wrong. Open and close the same tag
  • Text in the head. Everything you want to SEE goes in the body
