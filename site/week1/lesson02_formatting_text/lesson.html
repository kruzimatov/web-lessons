Some words are more important. We can make them bold or *italic*.

Part 4: Special symbols

How do you show < on the page? The browser thinks it is the start of a tag! We use a special code instead.

CodeShows
&lt;< (less than)
&gt;> (greater than)
&amp;& (and)
&copy;© (copyright)

Show the tag <p> on the page, and write a copyright line with your name.

Which code shows < on the page?

Watch out!

  • Writing </br> or </hr>. <br> and <hr> have no closing tag
  • Pressing Enter many times for space. Use <br> or a new <p>
  • Wrong order: <strong><em>Hi</strong></em>. Close the inside tag first
  • Making everything bold. If everything is important, nothing is
  • Forgetting the ; in &lt;
