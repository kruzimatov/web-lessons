When I'm stuck
  1. Read my code slowly, line by line.
  2. Press Check my answer and look at the goals without a tick.
  3. Press Check my code and read what it says.
  4. Check that every tag that opens also closes.
  5. Look it up in My tags.
  6. Compare with the example in the lesson.
  7. Try Hint 1, then Hint 2.
  8. Still stuck? Ask your teacher.

Task 1 · Easy

Write a 4-line poem. Use <br> so every line starts on a new line.

  • The poem is in a p
  • 3 <br> for 4 lines
Hint 1

<br> goes at the END of each line.

Hint 2

Do not put <br> after the last line.

Task 2 · Medium

Make the important words strong and the feeling words em.

  • Important words in strong
  • A feeling word in em
Hint 1

Important words: <strong>. Feeling words: <em>.

Hint 2

"Wash your hands" is important. "so" is a feeling word.

Task 3 · Find the bug

There are 3 mistakes. Fix them.

  • A <br> new line, not </br>
  • Tags close in the right order
  • The 2 in H2O goes down: sub, not sup
  • H<sub>2</sub>O uses sub
Hint 1

Does <br> have a closing tag? Which box closes first?

Hint 2

<br> not </br>. Close em before strong. The 2 in H₂O goes DOWN.

Task 4 · Medium

Toy shop: the car was 20$, now 15$. Cross out the old price and highlight the new one. Then write 5² = 25 with sup.

  • The old price is crossed out: <del>20$</del>
  • The new price is highlighted: <mark>15$</mark>
  • 5² written as 5<sup>2</sup>
Hint 1

Old price: <del>. New price: <mark>.

Hint 2

5² is 5<sup>2</sup>.

Task 5 · Mini project

"Fun Facts" page: an h1, 4 facts, at least 5 different tags from today, an <hr>, and a copyright line at the bottom.

  • An h1
  • 4 facts
  • 5 different tags from today
  • A line across: <hr>
  • A copyright line: &copy;
Hint 1

Count your tags: strong, em, sub, sup, mark, hr, small...

Hint 2

End with <p><small>&copy; 2026 Your name</small></p>.
