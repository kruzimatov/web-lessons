When I'm stuck
  1. Read my code slowly, line by line.
  2. Press Check my code and read what it says.
  3. Check that every tag that opens also closes.
  4. Look it up in My tags.
  5. Compare with the example in the lesson.
  6. Try Hint 1, then Hint 2.
  7. Still stuck? Ask your teacher.

Task 1 · Easy

Start the page: the skeleton with the title "About Me" and an <h1> with your name.

Hint 1

Look at the skeleton in the lesson.

Hint 2

The title goes in head, the h1 goes in body.

Task 2 · Medium

Add two paragraphs about you. Make your age strong and one word em. Then an <hr>.

Hint 1

Age in <strong>, one word in <em>.

Hint 2

The <hr> comes after the second </p>.

Task 3 · Find the bug

Fix the 4 mistakes in this page.

Hint 1

There are 4: count the h1s, check the order, check the list, check the end.

Hint 2

Second h1 becomes p. Close strong before p. Dogs needs li. Add </body>.

Task 4 · Medium

Add "My favorite things" (h2 + dots list of 3) and "My top 3" (h2 + numbers list).

Hint 1

Favorite things: <ul>. Top 3: <ol>.

Hint 2

Each part starts with an <h2>.
