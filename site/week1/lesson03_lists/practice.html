When I'm stuck
  1. Read my code slowly, line by line.
  2. Press Check my answer and look at the goals without a tick.
  3. Press Check my code and read what it says.
  4. Check that every tag that opens also closes.
  5. Look it up in My tags.
  6. Compare with the example in the lesson.
  7. Try Hint 1, then Hint 2.
  8. Still stuck? Ask your teacher.

Task 1 · Easy

Make a list (dots) of 4 things in your school bag.

  • A dots list: ul
  • 4 things: 4 li
Hint 1

Dots list = <ul>.

Hint 2

Every thing in the bag is one <li>.

Task 2 · Medium

Top 5 games or cartoons, with numbers. Make number 1 strong.

  • A numbers list: ol
  • 5 items: 5 li
  • Number 1 is strong
Hint 1

Numbers list = <ol>.

Hint 2

Number 1: <li><strong>Game</strong></li>.

Task 3 · Find the bug

There are 3 mistakes. Fix them.

  • Every animal is in its own <li>
  • </ul> ends with >
Hint 1

Is every item inside <li>? Does every tag close and end with >?

Hint 2

Dogs needs <li>. Fish needs </li>. The last line needs >.

Task 4 · Medium

Animals list: Pets (cat, dog) and Wild animals (lion, bear). Each group has its own small list.

  • Pets and Wild animals each get a small list
  • 4 animals in the small lists
Hint 1

The small list goes INSIDE the <li>, before </li>.

Hint 2

<li>Pets <ul><li>Cat</li><li>Dog</li></ul></li>

Task 5 · Mini project

Recipe page: an h1 with a food name, an h2 "You need" with a dots list, an h2 "Steps" with a numbers list of 4 steps.

  • An h1 with a food name
  • An h2 "You need", then an h2 "Steps"
  • A dots list of things you need
  • A numbers list of 4 steps
Hint 1

Things you need: no order, so <ul>. Steps: order matters, so <ol>.

Hint 2

h1, h2, ul, h2, ol.
