Part 1: Your first link

A link is a word you can click to go to another page. Links are what make the web a WEB: pages joined together.

PartCodeMeaning
Tag<a>a = anchor, a link

Change the link text. Then hold the mouse over the link: it becomes a hand.

Which part says WHERE the link goes?

Part 2: Open in a new tab

Add target="_blank" and the link opens in a new tab. Your page stays open.

Without targetWith target="_blank"
Opens in the same tabOpens in a new tab
Your page goes awayYour page stays open

In our live editor, always use target="_blank". Then the link opens properly in a new tab.

Make 2 links that open in a new tab: one to Wikipedia, one to NASA.

Watch out!

  • Forgetting https:// at the start of a website address
  • Forgetting the quotes: href=wikipedia.org must be href="https://www.wikipedia.org"
  • Forgetting </a>: then the whole page becomes a link!
  • Link text "click here". Say where the link goes instead
