⏺ The new teacher panel is live. Open https://webb-lessons.vercel.app/teacher
  and press Cmd+Shift+R once.

  The two problems
  - "Teacher panel" kept showing after login: that link sat next to your name
    even while you were on the panel. Now the navbar has a normal Teacher link,
    underlined when you're on that page.
  - "See work" wasn't working properly: his work was on the server (practice for
    lessons 2 and 3, homework for lessons 1 and 2, game code). The old panel
    showed it as collapsed rows under the table, with no task text and the same
    time on everything. It has been replaced.

  How the new panel works
  - Students column (left): name, "Online now" in green if active in the last 5
    minutes, otherwise "Active 20 min ago", plus stars and diamonds. + Add 
    student is at the top. The panel refreshes every 30 seconds, and there's a
    manual Refresh button.
  - Student page: six tiles at the top: practice tasks done, homework tasks
    done, game levels, stars, diamonds and best race.
  - Progress grid: one row per lesson (1 to 16), with squares for Practice 1 to
    5 and Homework 1 to 3:
    - 🟩 Done (changed from the starter code, no mistakes)
    - 🟨 Has mistakes (the same checker the student sees)
    - ⬜ Not changed yet
    - Dashed: empty

    Click any square to open it.
  - Tabs: Homework and Practice list tasks by lesson, with the task text, status
    and time. Game shows all 14 levels with his stars, his cars and which
    homework earned diamonds. Playground shows his free page.
  - Work viewer: a large window with the task (in yellow), any mistakes with
    line numbers, his code and a live view of his page. Previous / Next (or the
    arrow keys) step through all his work.
  - Manage: New password and Delete open proper windows. Delete clearly warns
    that it erases all his work. Add student shows errors inside the window,
    like "Password needs at least 8 characters". Change my password is in the
    sidebar.

  I tested all of this locally with sample work in Chrome, in light and dark
  mode: the grid colours, viewer order, mistakes list, adding (including the
  short-password error), deleting with confirmation, and refresh. There were no
  JS errors, and the checker and server tests pass. I couldn't click through the
  live site myself because Vercel's bot check blocks my headless browser, so
  please confirm it looks right there.
